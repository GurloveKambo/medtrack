💊 MediTrack

User Manual · Quick Start Guide

📖 What is MediTrack?

MediTrack helps you manage a large stock of medicines bought in bulk from different brands. It tells you which box to open first, how many days of supply you have, and when you'll run out — all calculated automatically.

💡 Your father's doctor prescribes a salt (e.g. Metformin 500mg). You may buy it as Glucophage, Metlong, or a generic — different brand, same medicine. MediTrack tracks them all together under one prescription.

🧠 Three things to understand

📋 Prescription — what the doctor wrote.
The salt/generic name + when to take it (e.g. Morning every day, Bedtime Mon–Fri).
📦 Purchase — what you actually bought.
Brand name, lot number, expiry date, quantity on hand. One entry per box or strip.
📅 Schedule — per slot (Morning / Noon / Evening / Bedtime), pick which days of the week. Can vary — e.g. Morning only on weekdays.

🚀 Getting started

1

Go to 📋 Rx tab → tap + Add → enter the salt name (e.g. "Amlodipine 5mg"), condition, doctor's name, and set the weekly schedule.

2

Go to 📦 Purchases tab → tap + Add → select the prescription, enter the brand name, lot number, expiry date, and quantity on hand today. Repeat for every box you have.

3

Go to 🏠 Today — your daily schedule appears automatically. Each medicine shows which brand to take from and when supply runs out.

⚠ If you bought the same salt from two different brands or sources, add them as separate purchase entries. The app will order them by expiry — open the one expiring first.

🗂️ The four tabs

🏠 Today
Your father's daily medicine schedule grouped by Morning / Noon / Evening / Bedtime. Shows which brand to take, units left, and the exact run-out date. Red alert appears when supply drops under 30 days.
📋 Rx (Prescriptions)
One entry per prescription your doctor wrote. Tap any to see all purchases linked to it, supply summary, and which box is currently open.
📦 Purchases
All boxes/strips you've bought, sorted by earliest expiry. Search by brand name or lot number. Filter by prescription.
⚙️ Settings
Switch tracking mode, export/import backup.

⚙️ Tracking modes

Auto tracking (assume taken) Recommended
Stock is calculated automatically from the schedule and the date you counted. No daily input needed. Just update the quantity in Purchases whenever you physically count the stock (e.g. when opening a new box).
Manual confirm
Today screen shows a ✓ Taken button for each dose. Stock reduces only when you tap it. Use this if doses are sometimes skipped.

📦 Which box to open first?

Inside any prescription, purchases are sorted by earliest expiry first. The app marks the current one as 🟢 Open and the next as 📦 Next. Always open the earliest-expiring box so nothing goes to waste.

💡 When you open a new box, you don't need to do anything in the app — in Auto mode it calculates automatically. In Manual mode, the next box becomes active once the current one runs out.

🛒 Managing bulk stock

1

Add one purchase entry per box or strip — even if they're the same brand. Different lot numbers or expiry dates = separate entries.

2

Enter the quantity on hand and the date you counted it. Auto mode calculates consumption from that date.

3

When you recount stock (e.g. monthly), just edit the purchase entry and update the quantity and count date. The app recalculates immediately.

💾 Backup

Go to ⚙️ Settings → Export Backup to download a JSON file. Save it to Google Drive or WhatsApp it to yourself. To restore, tap Import from backup and select the file.

⚠ All data is stored only on this device. If you clear the browser or switch phones, use the export/import to move your data.
